1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy describes how TruePrice collects, uses, and protects information when you use our mobile application. By using the app, you agree to the practices outlined in this policy.

2. Data Collection

TruePrice does not collect any personal information directly from users.

However, the app may access or process the following data to provide its core functionality:

  • Network access: to fetch stock prices and financial data via secure APIs
  • Device language and region settings: to localize app content and support language switching
  • Usage data (anonymized): may be collected via Google Play Console or crash reporting tools to help improve the app

🔒 Note: No personal identifiers (like name, email, contacts, or device ID) are collected, stored, or transmitted by this app.

3. Data Sharing

TruePrice does not sell or rent your data. We do not share user data with third parties except:

  • API providers (e.g., financial data services) for retrieving public stock information
  • Analytics services (e.g., Google Play Console) for usage statistics and crash reporting

All data shared is non-personal and anonymized.

4. Permissions Used

TruePrice may request access to:

  • Network state: to check for internet availability
  • Storage (optional): only if exporting data or screenshots is supported in future versions

5. Children’s Privacy

TrueStock is not directed to children under 13 and does not knowingly collect any personal information from them. If you believe we’ve unintentionally collected data from a child, please contact us for immediate deletion.

6. Your Rights

Since no personal data is collected or stored, you are not required to register, opt-in, or manage any user account with us.

If this changes in future versions, users will be informed and offered privacy controls accordingly.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with the updated effective date.
